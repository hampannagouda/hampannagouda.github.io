AI Stock Prediction System

Forecasts a stock's next-day closing price with an LSTM sequence model. Before committing to deep learning, I baselined a Random Forest on the same lagged-feature data — the LSTM pipeline below is what I kept after that comparison.

Engineering decisions

  • 15 engineered features: SMA/EMA trend indicators, RSI & MACD momentum, lagged returns
  • Two-layer LSTM (64 → 32 units) with 20% dropout between layers to curb overfitting
  • EarlyStopping + ReduceLROnPlateau callbacks instead of a fixed epoch count
  • MinMaxScaler fit independently on features and target for stable convergence

Why it's here

  • Full pipeline ownership: data ingestion → features → model → serving, not just a notebook
  • Compared a baseline model before choosing the final architecture
  • Trained model and scalers are committed and directly loadable for inference
